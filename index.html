River Intelligence Workflow

1. Confirm Conditions Use official Henthorn telemetry first: river level, movement and timestamp.
2. Check Editorial Intelligence Use Trout & Salmon as Tier 1 magazine intelligence for river mentions, current-season reports and wider salmon activity context.
3. Seek RFCA Data Investigate RFCA water quality, temperature, fish-counter and association intelligence.
4. Seek Operator / Beat Intelligence Use named operators, beats and fishery reports only when dated and traceable.
5. Block Weak Claims Exclude forum rumours, undated claims, old-season figures and untraceable social media posts unless independently corroborated.

Intelligence Fusion Rules

A source can influence the live intelligence layer only when it has:

• identifiable origin
• current-season relevance where catch-related
• timestamp or publication date
• traceable link, report, page or photographed source
• non-speculative wording

Otherwise it remains blocked or excluded.
