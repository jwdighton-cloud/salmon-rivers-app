RFCA acquisition rule
RFCA is the first real acquisition target
RFCA is being treated as the first priority source to investigate for fish counter data, current-season catch updates, and the water temperature dashboard feed. No RFCA-derived catch, counter, or temperature value is displayed until the source is verified.

Source Investigation Priority Order
1
RFCA fish counter / catch update data
Highest-value first target because it may connect fish movement, catch updates, authority context, and counter references.
2
RFCA temperature dashboard feed
Important Henthorn/Ribble condition signal. No value may be shown until a stable, traceable feed is verified.
3
Ribble Rivers Trust monitoring / counter references
Investigate monitoring reports, fish passage references, and any counter-related current-season material.
4
Operator / fishery catch reports
Target beat-level salmon and sea trout reports where source, date, location, and species can be verified.
5
Salmon Fishing Forum manual review
Useful for awareness and leads only. Forum content does not become operational data without independent confirmation.
