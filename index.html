Manual Verification Workflow
1
Find source
Identify the exact page, post, report, dashboard, fishery update, or counter source.
2
Confirm current season
The source must relate to the current 2026 season only.
3
Confirm species
Salmon and sea trout must be separately labelled. Mixed or unclear totals stay blocked.
4
Confirm river or beat
The report must identify river, tributary, beat, fishery, or counter location.
5
Confirm value
Only exact reported catch numbers, fish counter values, or telemetry readings can be used.
6
Record source and date
Store source link, report date, update timestamp, and confidence status.
7
Display only when verified
Only then can a value enter the live intelligence layer.
No value moves from candidate source to operational display without this verification chain.
