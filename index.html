Trout & Salmon Magazine Intelligence

Trout & Salmon fishing reports are now treated as a Tier 1 Magazine Intelligence Source.

They are not telemetry and they are not official fish-counter data. They are used as curated river-by-river intelligence, trend evidence, catch-report discovery and regional salmon activity context.
Source Class Magazine Intelligence — Tier 1
Permitted Use River mentions, reported activity, dated catch references, regional trend indicators and source discovery.
Blocked Use Cannot be treated as exact telemetry, live counter data, or official catch totals unless explicitly stated and traceable.
Current App Handling Source recognised and classified. No magazine figures are displayed until directly verified from a current report.

Verification Rules

A source can enter the live intelligence layer only if it has:

• identifiable origin
• current-season relevance
• timestamp or publication date
• traceable link, report, page or photographed source
• non-speculative wording

Otherwise it stays out.

Platform Direction

Salmon Rivers Intelligence is a verified information-seeking terminal.

It should pull, classify and present external evidence, not depend on user-entered data.

Current architecture:

• verified telemetry layer
• Tier 1 magazine intelligence layer
• source-seeking layer
• acquisition investigation layer
• verification control layer
• excluded-data protection layer

Expansion beyond the Ribble remains blocked until the Ribble source model is stable.
